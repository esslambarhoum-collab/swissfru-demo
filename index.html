Working demonstration for SwissFru, prepared by Apeiron Advisory. Products and prices follow the September 2026 lists. Companies and orders are sample data. Changes stay on this device.

The factory, the warehouse, shipments and customers, in one place.

Pick a door. Everything is connected: what a distributor orders shows up in the office, and what the driver taps shows up on the owner's phone.

Try this in five minutes

  1. Sign in as the owner. Four big numbers and a short list of what needs you.
  2. Open Sales, then Expo mode. Take a visitor's details and an order on the spot.
  3. Open Orders. Record the deposit. The factory sees what to make.
  4. Open the driver link. Tap Picked up, then On the way.
  5. Open the distributor portal. Order from the catalogue and pay by card.
Tap any ? button inside the system for a plain-English explanation of that screen.

Who is signing in?

In the real system this is an email, a password and a code on the phone. Here, pick a person. Each one sees a different system.
